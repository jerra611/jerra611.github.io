I offer compassionate, personalized therapy services to support you through life's challenges. Whether you're seeking individual support or couples counseling, I'm here to help.

## Individual Therapy

Individual therapy provides a safe space to explore your thoughts, feelings, and experiences. I work with clients on a variety of concerns, including:

- Anxiety and stress management
- Depression and mood difficulties
- Life transitions and adjustments
- Self-esteem and personal growth
- Relationship concerns
- Work-life balance
- Grief and loss

## Couples Therapy

Relationships require care and attention to thrive. Couples therapy can help you and your partner:

- Improve communication and connection
- Navigate conflicts more effectively
- Rebuild trust and intimacy
- Understand each other's needs and perspectives
- Strengthen your relationship foundation

## Areas of Focus

I have particular interest and training in working with:

- Young adults navigating identity and independence
- Relationship difficulties and communication patterns
- Anxiety and stress-related concerns
- Life transitions and major changes
