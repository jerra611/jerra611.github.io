## Ready to Begin?

Taking the first step toward therapy is often the hardest part. I'm here to make the process as easy and comfortable as possible.

---

## How It Works

### 1. Reach Out

Contact me by [email](mailto:jerra@jerratherapy.com) or phone [(951) 217-4282](tel:+19512174282) to express your interest in therapy. Let me know a bit about what you're looking for and any questions you have.

### 2. Free Consultation

We'll schedule a brief 15-minute phone consultation (no charge) to:
- Discuss what brings you to therapy
- See if we're a good fit
- Answer any questions about my approach, rates, or logistics
- Determine if individual, couples, or a combination would be most helpful

There's no pressure or obligation—this is simply a chance for us to connect and see if working together feels right.

### 3. Schedule Your First Session

If we both feel it's a good fit, we'll schedule your first appointment. I'll send you:
- Intake paperwork (completed securely online)
- Information about telehealth platform and how to access sessions
- Details about fees, cancellation policy, and what to expect

### 4. First Session

Your first session is an opportunity to:
- Share your story and what's been challenging
- Explore your goals for therapy
- Discuss how I might be able to help
- Begin building our therapeutic relationship

Sessions are 50-55 minutes and conducted via secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform.

---

## What to Expect in Therapy

**Frequency:** Most clients meet weekly, especially in the beginning. We can adjust based on your needs and progress.

**Format:** I offer individual therapy, couples therapy, and a combination of both for couples who want deeper work.

**Approach:** I use evidence-based methods like Emotionally Focused Therapy, Family Systems Theory, and Relational Life Therapy, tailored to your unique situation.

**Your Role:** Therapy is collaborative. You're the expert on your own life—I'm here to help you access your strengths and navigate challenges.

## Common Questions

**Do you take insurance?**
I am currently a private pay practice and do not accept insurance directly. However, I can provide you with a superbill (detailed receipt) that you can submit to your insurance company for potential out-of-network reimbursement.

**What are your rates?**
- 60-minute session: $150
- 90-minute session: $200
- 2-hour session: $250

Payment is due at the time of service and can be made via credit card, debit card, or HSA/FSA card.

**What if I need to cancel or reschedule?**
I require at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations made within 24 hours of your scheduled appointment will be charged 50% of the session fee. I understand that emergencies happen—please reach out and we can discuss your situation.

**How long will I be in therapy?**
This varies for everyone. Some clients find significant relief in a few months, while others benefit from longer-term support. We'll regularly check in about your progress and goals.

**What if I'm not sure if therapy is right for me?**
That's completely normal. The free consultation is designed to help you explore this question without any commitment.
