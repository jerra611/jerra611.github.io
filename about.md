## About Me

Are you stuck in the same painful cycles—repeated conflict, communication breakdowns, resentment, or growing disconnection? Maybe you love each other but struggle to feel heard, understood, or emotionally safe. Maybe you're an individual navigating anxiety, depression, trauma, or a difficult life transition. Therapy can be a place to slow down, understand what's happening beneath the surface, and find a path forward.

### My Journey to Therapy

Before becoming a licensed therapist, I spent 15 years as a hairstylist at [Deseo Salon](https://deseosalondenver.com) in Denver. Those years taught me the profound importance of truly listening, creating safe spaces for vulnerability, and understanding that transformation—whether it's a new look or a new perspective—requires trust, patience, and collaboration. The skills I developed in building genuine connections and helping people feel seen carried forward into my transition to therapy, where I now support individuals and couples through their own transformations.

I'm grateful to still be connected with the Deseo community, and I bring that same warmth, authenticity, and commitment to meeting people exactly where they are.

## My Approach

I offer collaborative, individualized therapy for couples and individuals in Denver and throughout Colorado. With couples, I help uncover the patterns beneath the conflict, rebuild emotional safety, strengthen communication skills, and reconnect. With individuals, I support trauma processing, anxiety and depression treatment, emotional regulation, and navigating life transitions with greater clarity and resilience.

I believe we are all inherently resilient, even when that part of you feels buried beneath pain and overwhelm. I'll meet you where you are, move at your pace, and create a safe, nonjudgmental space to feel, process, and grow.

### Therapeutic Orientation

My therapeutic approach is integrative, drawing from evidence-based practices while honoring each client's unique journey. I incorporate elements from several therapeutic modalities, including:

- **Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT)** - Strengthening emotional bonds and attachment in relationships
- **Systems Theory** - Understanding how relationships and family dynamics influence our well-being
- **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)** - Identifying and shifting unhelpful thought patterns
- **Trauma-Informed Care** - Creating safety and supporting healing from past wounds
- **Mindfulness-Based Approaches** - Cultivating present-moment awareness and self-compassion

### What to Expect

Therapy is a collaborative journey. In our work together, you can expect:

- A safe, confidential, and non-judgmental space
- Compassionate listening and genuine support
- Practical tools and strategies tailored to your needs
- Respect for your values, culture, and experiences
- A focus on your strengths and inherent resilience

### Ready for Something to Change?

I'd love to connect. Whether you're seeking couples counseling, individual therapy for anxiety or depression, trauma therapy, or support through a major life transition, I'm here to help you find your way forward.
