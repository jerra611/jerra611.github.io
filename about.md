## My Approach

My therapeutic approach is integrative, drawing from evidence-based practices while honoring each client's unique journey. I believe in the power of the therapeutic relationship and work to create a warm, authentic connection that fosters growth and healing.

### Therapeutic Orientation

I incorporate elements from several therapeutic modalities, including:

- **Systems Theory** - Understanding how relationships and family dynamics influence our well-being
- **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)** - Identifying and shifting unhelpful thought patterns
- **Mindfulness-Based Approaches** - Cultivating present-moment awareness and self-compassion
- **Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT)** - Strengthening emotional bonds in relationships

### What to Expect

Therapy is a collaborative journey. In our work together, you can expect:

- A safe, confidential, and non-judgmental space
- Compassionate listening and genuine support
- Practical tools and strategies tailored to your needs
- Respect for your values, culture, and experiences
- A focus on your strengths and resilience
